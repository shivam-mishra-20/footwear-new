# Future Feature Ideas & Enhancements

## 🤖 Automation Features

### Inventory Management

- **Auto-Reorder Alerts**: Automatic notifications when stock falls below a threshold
- **Predictive Stock Analysis**: ML-based predictions for stock requirements based on sales trends
- **Bulk Import/Export**: CSV/Excel import for adding multiple products at once
- **Barcode Generation**: Auto-generate barcodes for new products
- **Stock Movement History**: Track when products were added, sold, or restocked
- **Low Stock Dashboard Widget**: Visual alerts for products running out of stock
- **Expiry Date Tracking**: For seasonal items, track when to discount or remove stock

### Sales & POS

- **Quick Sale Templates**: Save common product combinations for faster checkout
- **Customer Loyalty Program**: Points-based system with automatic discounts
- **Digital Receipt via Email**: Send invoices directly to customer email
- **Split Payment Support**: Allow multiple payment methods in one transaction
- **Returns & Refunds Management**: Track returned items and process refunds
- **Gift Cards/Vouchers**: Create and redeem gift cards
- **Tax Calculation**: Automatic GST/tax calculation based on product categories
- **Offline Mode**: Work without internet and sync when connection is restored

### Reporting & Analytics

- **Automated Daily/Weekly/Monthly Reports**: Email summary reports automatically
- **Profit Margin Analysis**: Calculate and display profit per product/sale
- **Sales Forecasting**: Predict future sales based on historical data
- **Customer Purchase History**: Track what customers buy over time
- **Top Selling Hours**: Identify peak business hours for staffing optimization
- **Category Performance**: Which product categories sell best
- **Seasonal Trends**: Identify seasonal patterns in sales
- **Export to Excel**: Export reports in various formats (PDF, Excel, CSV)

## 👥 Customer Management

### Customer Features

- **Customer Database**: Store customer details, purchase history
- **Customer Groups**: Segment customers (VIP, Regular, New)
- **Birthday/Anniversary Reminders**: Automatic discount offers
- **Customer Purchase Patterns**: What they usually buy
- **Credit/Account System**: Allow trusted customers to buy on credit
- **SMS Notifications**: Order confirmations, payment reminders
- **Customer Feedback System**: Collect reviews and ratings

## 📱 Communication & Marketing

- **Social Media Integration**: Share products on social platforms
- **QR Code Payments**: Generate QR codes for UPI payments
